Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This policy explains how TallyNest (小记), developed by Zihao Huang, and this support website handle information. The app stores records locally and does not require a TallyNest account. You can choose to enable iCloud sync. App Store versions that offer Pro use Apple In-App Purchase.

1. Records in the app

TallyNest stores the information you enter on your device: tasks, dates, reminders, lists, notes, subtasks, account names, currencies, balances, financial entries and links between records. This information supports the task and finance features. Preferences and local backups are also stored locally.

The app does not upload these records to a developer-operated server, connect to your bank accounts or make real payments or transfers. Accounts and transfers in TallyNest record financial information you enter manually.

2. iCloud sync

When you enable iCloud, TallyNest uses Apple's private CloudKit database to sync compatible devices signed in to the same Apple Account. Synced information includes tasks, lists, week titles and finances: dates, notes, subtasks, accounts, balances, entries, categories, budgets, recurring rules, linked records, and change and deletion information needed for sync. This uses your iCloud storage, not a developer-operated server.

The app checks iCloud availability and syncs while it is open. You can turn sync off in Settings and continue using your local data. Turning sync off does not delete existing cloud copies. Sync and recovery copies do not replace independent backups. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by its privacy policy.

3. In-App Purchase

In App Store versions that offer Pro, Apple handles payment, subscriptions, refunds and purchase restoration. TallyNest uses StoreKit to verify transaction and entitlement information provided by Apple, such as product identifiers, transaction identifiers, expiry dates, and refund or revocation status. This information unlocks Pro, updates expired access and restores purchases.

We do not receive your full payment card details or ask you to send the developer your Apple Account password. The app does not upload tasks, financial records or transaction credentials to a developer-operated server for purchase verification. See App Store & Privacy for Apple's handling of purchase data. The Apple Standard Licence Agreement provides the terms of use.

4. Exchange-rate requests

When automatic rate updates are enabled, or you request rates manually, TallyNest retrieves public reference rates from the European Central Bank over HTTPS. Requests do not include your tasks, account balances, entries or notes. Service providers and network infrastructure may receive technical information normally included in network requests, such as your IP address. You can manage automatic updates in finance settings.

Reference rates are for recording and estimation, not actual bank settlement rates. See the ECB website privacy statement.

5. Notifications and file access

With your permission, TallyNest uses local system notifications for task reminders. Notifications may contain task titles. You can turn off or adjust notifications in system settings.

Import and export use the system file picker to access the files or locations you select. The app processes those files for the requested operation and does not send your backups to the developer automatically.

6. Backups, retention and deletion

You can export your data using the backup feature and delete records in the app. Items in Recently Deleted remain until restored, permanently deleted or removed under applicable retention rules. Historical backups may still contain deleted records; manage existing backups and exported files if you also want to remove those copies.

Local automatic backups are subject to retention rules and available storage. They do not guarantee a copy of every change. Exported full backups are not password-encrypted, so store them somewhere you trust. If you save a file to iCloud Drive or another cloud drive, that service handles it under its own settings and policies.

7. Contacting support

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to respond, investigate the issue and communicate with you. Please avoid sending unrelated personal or financial information. Support information is retained while needed to handle the issue and necessary follow-up. Contact the email address below to request deletion of support materials you have sent.

8. Advertising, analytics and this website

The app writes some technical events and errors to the device’s system logs to help diagnose saving, sync and purchase issues. It does not include a separate developer-operated log-upload service. Apple may process diagnostics and provide developers with crash or test reports through its own services, such as system analytics sharing or TestFlight. Apple’s related settings and privacy policies apply; this is separate from the app continuously uploading logs to a developer-operated server.

The app does not include advertising SDKs, third-party behavioural analytics or cross-app advertising tracking. This website does not set its own analytics cookies, contain ads or tracking scripts, or offer an online form to upload financial records or backups.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process access logs, including IP addresses, request times, paths and browser information, to provide and secure the website. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Email links open your email app; we receive your feedback only if you choose to send it.

9. Policy updates

If our information-handling practices change, we will update this policy and provide notice or seek consent where needed. The date at the top shows the latest update.
