COMMON QUESTIONS

Start here

Where are my tasks and finances stored?

Your tasks, lists and finances are stored on your device. You can enable iCloud in Settings to sync compatible app versions through Apple's private cloud storage using the same Apple Account. Sync runs while the app is open; you can still edit offline. Sync does not replace a backup, so export a full backup regularly.

How do I back up or restore my data?

Open Settings, find Backup and Restore, and choose Export Full Backup. Use the same section to select a backup to restore, and read the recovery instructions before continuing. Exporting your current data first is recommended. A finance CSV helps you review entries but does not replace a full backup.

Can I recover a deleted task or entry?

Check Recently Deleted in the relevant section first. If the item was permanently deleted, check automatic backups in Settings or a full backup you previously exported. A backup may not include your latest changes. Check its date before restoring.

Why didn't I receive a reminder?

Check that the task has a reminder and that its time is still in the future. Then check that notifications for TallyNest are allowed in system settings. Focus modes, notification settings and device state may affect delivery. If the problem continues, send us the task time, reminder settings and system version.

Why aren't transfers counted as spending? Are exchange rates exact?

Moving money between your accounts is not the same as spending it; record any fees separately. Foreign-currency asset totals use reference exchange rates and are estimates. They may differ from your bank's settlement amounts. Use the actual amounts paid, received and charged by your bank when recording transactions.

How do tasks and finances work together?

You can link entries to a task, such as flight and accommodation costs for a trip. Open Linked Entries in a task's details to review these entries or add another one.

What is included in Free and Pro?

The free plan has no task or entry count limit. It includes reminders, repeating tasks, day/week/month views and interactive scheduling, linked task entries, iCloud sync and backups. Statistics cover this month and the previous month.

Free limits are 5 lists in total (including 3 preset lists), 5 active accounts, 2 active books, 3 ongoing recurring bills and a budget for 1 book. Archived books with budgets also count toward the budget limit. Pro removes these creation limits and adds earlier cross-period trends, the statistics calendar and category history. Monthly, annual and lifetime plans provide the same Pro features.

How do I use Pro on both iPhone and Mac?

App Store versions that offer Pro use Apple In-App Purchase. Use the same purchasing Apple Account in the App Store on your iPhone, iPad and Mac, then choose Restore Purchases in the Pro section of TallyNest's Settings. Your purchasing account may differ from your iCloud sync account. Restoring purchases checks your Pro access; it does not import or replace your tasks and finances.

Available plans, local prices and renewal periods are shown on the in-app Apple purchase screen. TestFlight test purchases are not production purchases.

What happens when my subscription expires?

Your existing tasks, entries, accounts, books and recurring bills remain available to view, edit and export. Existing items above a free limit remain usable; only further over-limit creation is restricted. Existing budgets remain editable too. Day, week and month views, including interactive scheduling, stay free. Statistics for this month and the previous month remain available; earlier cross-period trends, the statistics calendar and category history require active Pro access.

You can cancel automatic renewal through Apple's subscription management. Access continues until the end of the paid period. A lifetime purchase has no subscription renewal. Buying lifetime does not automatically cancel an existing subscription. Cancel it separately in Apple subscription management to avoid further renewal charges.

What if a purchase or restore fails?

Check your connection and App Store account, then retry or choose Restore Purchases. A purchase awaiting approval unlocks after approval; you do not need to buy repeatedly. If you still need help, send the app version and error message. Do not send passwords, verification codes or complete payment details.

Help us understand what happened

Include the app version, system version, steps to reproduce the issue and screenshots with personal information hidden. Please do not send passwords, verification codes or full bank card numbers. There is no need to attach your full financial records or backup unless it is needed and you choose to share it.
